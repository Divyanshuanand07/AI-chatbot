## Scope

This policy covers cancellation of a vehicle purchase order and the refund of
any amount already collected from the customer. It applies to all channels —
app, website, hub walk-in, telesales and partner.

## Who can cancel an order

- The customer may cancel at any point before delivery is completed.
- An operations agent may cancel on the customer's recorded request.
- An operations manager may cancel without customer request where the vehicle
  has become unavailable, the inspection disclosed a material defect, or the
  order has been inactive for more than thirty days with no customer response.

Cancellation after delivery is not a cancellation; it falls under the seven-day
return window and is handled by the Returns team.

## Refund timelines

Refunds must be initiated within **two working days** of the cancellation being
recorded. The credit reaching the customer depends on the original method:

- UPI and net banking — five to seven working days from initiation.
- Credit and debit card — seven to ten working days, because the issuing bank
  controls the final credit.
- Loan disbursement already received — returned to the lender, not the
  customer, within seven working days. The customer's loan account must be
  closed by the lender; we confirm closure in writing.

The overall internal SLA is that no refund should remain in a pending state for
more than **seven days**. A refund pending beyond seven days is an SLA breach
and must be escalated to the Finance lead.

## Deductions

The token or booking amount is fully refundable except in these cases:

- The customer cancels after the RC transfer has been filed with the RTO. The
  actual RTO charges incurred are deducted; the balance is refunded.
- The customer cancels after two failed delivery attempts caused by the
  customer being unavailable. A logistics fee of two thousand rupees applies.

No deduction applies where Cars24 cancelled the order, where the loan was
rejected, where the vehicle was found defective on re-inspection, or where the
vehicle became unavailable.

## What to tell the customer

Always give the customer three things: the amount being refunded, the method it
will be credited to, and the expected date range. Never promise a specific date
for a card refund, because the issuing bank controls it.

If a refund is already past the seven-day SLA, acknowledge the delay
explicitly, give a firm revised date, and record the commitment against the
order.

## Records

Every cancellation must have a recorded reason against the order. "Customer
request" alone is not sufficient — capture the underlying reason (chose another
vehicle, loan rejected, price concern, delivery delay, defect found), because
these drive the monthly cancellation analysis.
