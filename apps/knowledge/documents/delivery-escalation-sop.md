## Booking a delivery slot

A delivery slot is booked once the order is ready for delivery: payment
settled, mandatory documents verified, and KYC verified. An order sitting in
"ready for delivery" with no slot booked is a pure process gap — everything is
done and nobody has called the customer.

Slots are two-hour windows between 10:00 and 20:00. Confirm the slot, the full
address and the pincode with the customer on a call, not over chat, because
address errors are the leading cause of failed attempts.

## Failed attempts

Record every failed attempt with a reason. The common reasons are the customer
being unreachable, the customer not being at the address, the address being
incorrect or incomplete, and the customer refusing delivery.

**After two failed attempts, stop dispatching.** A third run costs more than
the call it takes to prevent it. Instead:

1. Call the customer and establish why the previous attempts failed.
2. Re-confirm the address and pincode field by field.
3. Book a new slot only once the customer has confirmed availability.
4. Where the customer has been unreachable across both attempts, escalate to
   the Order Operations agent who owns the order — logistics cannot resolve an
   unreachable customer.

Where two attempts failed because the customer was unavailable, a logistics fee
applies on cancellation. See the Refund and Cancellation Policy.

## Overdue deliveries

The promised delivery date is a commitment to the customer. When it passes
without delivery:

- Establish the real blocker before contacting the customer. An overdue date
  is almost always a symptom of an upstream problem — unverified documents, an
  unsettled payment, a pending RC transfer — and the customer will ask what
  the actual hold-up is.
- Give one firm revised date. Do not give a range, and do not give a date you
  are not confident of; a second missed date escalates automatically.
- Record the reason for the delay against the order.

## Escalation matrix

| Situation | Escalate to | Within |
|---|---|---|
| Two failed attempts | Order Operations agent owning the order | Same day |
| Delivery overdue by 3 days | Logistics hub lead | Same day |
| Delivery overdue by 7 days | Order Operations manager | Immediately |
| Vehicle damaged in transit | Hub QC lead and Order Operations manager | Immediately |
| Customer refuses delivery at the door | Order Operations manager | Immediately |

## Completing delivery

Delivery is complete only once the handover checklist is signed and the event
recorded against the order. Until that event exists, the order is still live
and will appear on the stalled report.
