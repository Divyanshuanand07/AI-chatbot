## Mandatory documents

For a self-funded purchase:

- Aadhaar card
- PAN card
- Address proof
- Driving licence

For a financed purchase, additionally:

- Bank statement for the last six months
- Salary slips for the last three months, or ITR for self-employed customers

The seller-side documents for the vehicle — registration certificate,
insurance policy, loan NOC where the vehicle was hypothecated, and Forms 29
and 30 — are collected against the vehicle, not the buyer's order.

## Collection window

The customer has **three days** from order creation to upload the mandatory
documents. Inside that window, missing documents are the normal state of a new
order and require no chase. Past three days, the order is blocked and the
customer must be contacted with the pending list.

## Verification SLA

Uploaded documents must be verified within **two working days**. A document
sitting in "uploaded" beyond two days is an internal SLA breach owned by the
Documentation team, not the customer, and must be escalated to the
verification queue owner rather than chased with the customer.

## Grounds for rejection

Reject a document only for one of these reasons, and always record the reason:

- Image is blurred or cropped so that details are unreadable.
- Name does not match across documents (most commonly PAN against Aadhaar).
- Document has expired.
- Address on the proof does not match the address given on the order.
- Document appears altered or inconsistent.

A rejection without a recorded reason cannot be actioned by the next agent and
will bounce back. This is the single most common cause of an order stalling on
documentation.

## Handling a rejection

1. Record the rejection reason against the document.
2. Contact the customer the same day, explaining specifically what is wrong and
   what to send instead. "Your document was rejected" without specifics
   guarantees a second rejection.
3. Where the issue is a name mismatch, tell the customer which two documents
   disagree and which one they should correct.
4. Re-verification is subject to the same two-day SLA from re-upload.

## Name mismatch between PAN and Aadhaar

This cannot be waived. The customer must either update the name on one of the
documents with the issuing authority, or provide a gazette notification or
marriage certificate evidencing the name change. Where a loan is involved, the
lender will also reject the application until this is resolved, so raise both
at once rather than sequentially.

## KYC outcomes

KYC verification is separate from individual document checks. A rejected KYC
blocks delivery outright and cannot be overridden by an agent or a manager; it
must be re-run once corrected documents are on file.
